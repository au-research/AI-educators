Dataspace Technical Coach

A guided module on the build side of dataspaces: using the IDS-RAM as your map, building or adopting a connector, expressing policies in ODRL on W3C standards, why the Dataspace Protocol exists and how to implement it — and proving your setup in a testbed before you commit. The coach asks before it tells; bring your real implementation. Rules and rulebooks? See the Governance Coach; fundamentals in the Dataspaces 101 Coach; open Q&A with the ARDC Dataspace Educator.

This module draws on the IDS-RAM 4.0, the Eclipse EDC stack and Dataspace Protocol, the W3C ODRL model, the IDSA IDS-testbed, and the prototype Australian Dataspace Testbed Service.
