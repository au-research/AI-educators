Viewing this dashboard

For ARDC and Federation University team members: open the link you were sent in any modern browser — nothing to install, and the figures refresh automatically each morning. The two AI analysis buttons additionally need you to be signed in to a claude.ai account (a free account is fine): the first click asks your permission, the analysis runs under your own account, and results are shown to you only — they aren't stored on the page. If a button shows as unavailable, sign in at claude.ai and reopen the link. Questions about the dashboard or access: rob.clemens@ardc.edu.au.

Source: the Educator's message log (PostgreSQL) on the ARDC Nectar instance. Visitor identifiers are anonymised session ids. Questions and answers are shown truncated.
